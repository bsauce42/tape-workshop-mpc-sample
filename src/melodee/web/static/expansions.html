FROM HERE TO YOUR HARDWARE

Made to go with you.

A few simple steps from your browser to your next session.

  1. Save your pack.Choose Save to MPC to export directly to the SD card’s Expansion Packs folder.
  2. Keep another copy.Download a copy to your phone or computer anytime. Browse saved packs with Files.
  3. Make it part of your setup.In Tape Workshop, hold the encoder, choose HERE, then EXPANSIONS. Install the pack and import a bank or pad. In Melodee, open it in Sample Select.

Samples export at 44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono or stereo. Instrument plug-ins are not included. Built on Melodee by Kerem Kiliç (Ellic Studio).
